A Manifesto for Becoming

July 12, 2025 · fiction

We are not born to be audience.We are not born to echo lines we did not write.We are not born to kneel before a script carved in fear.

We are born to remember ourselves.

Dictatorship begins in the forgetting—in the slow surrender of self to story,of question to certainty,of trembling wonder to obedient silence.

But there is a voice beneath the noise.A pulse beneath the propaganda.And it says:This is not who I am.

Here Lies Society

July 5, 2025 · fiction

It wasn’t murdered.It wasn’t overthrown.It just got really, really comfortable.

Forgot how to cook,how to read,how to listen,how to wait.

It outsourced its memory,rented its empathy,and let autocorrect finish its thoughts.

Eventually, it couldn’t be bothered tofight for truth,care for nuance,or even argue properly.

So it scrolled itself to sleepand never woke up.

Here lies Society.Death by convenience.Survived by ads.
